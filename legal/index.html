Packr

Packr is a packing-list app for travellers. You plan a trip, list what you are taking, decide which bag each item goes in, and see how heavy every bag is against its weight limit before you get to the airport.

What you can do

  • Build a list for each trip, with a quantity and a weight for every item.
  • Sort items into the places they travel: what you wear, your personal item, carry-on and checked bag. You can rename them and set each bag's own weight and limit.
  • Watch the weight of each bag against its limit as you add and move things.
  • Switch to pack mode and tick items off, including part of a quantity, as you put them in the bag.
  • Save templates for trips you take often and start a new list from one.
  • Print a list, or open it on your phone. Your trips follow you to any device when you sign in.

Access

Packr is a small personal project and is currently invite-only. You sign in with your Google account, so there is no separate password to manage.

Sign-in and your information

When you sign in, Packr receives only your name, your email address and an account identifier from Google. It does not get access to your email, files, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Read the Packr privacy policy for what is stored and how to delete it.
