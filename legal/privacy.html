Packr privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What Packr is

Packr is a small, invite-only packing-list app. This page explains what information it handles.

What Packr collects

  • From Google sign-in: your name, your email address and a unique account identifier. Packr asks for nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you enter: your trips, packing items, weights, bags and locations, templates, and the display name you choose.
  • A sign-in token: after you sign in, a random token is saved in your browser so you stay signed in. It expires after 30 days or when you sign out. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.

What Packr does not do

  • It does not sell or rent your information.
  • It does not show ads and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It does not read your Google email, files or contacts.

How the information is used

Your name and email identify your account. Your trips are stored so they are there when you sign in on another device. Nothing is used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored and who else is involved

  • Your account and trips are stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare. The app itself is served from GitHub Pages.
  • Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • To display the app, your browser loads code libraries from unpkg.com and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services can see your IP address when your browser asks them for these files. Packr does not send them anything about you or your trips.

Who can see it

Your trips are private to your account. The person who runs Packr could technically see stored data when maintaining the service, and will not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

Backups and earlier copies

Before Packr moved to its current database, its data was held in a different service. A read-only copy of that earlier data is kept for a short time after the move and is then deleted. The person who runs Packr also keeps occasional backups of the current database. Backups are replaced over time, so data you delete may remain in a backup for a limited period before it is removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the account menu and choose Delete Account. This permanently deletes your trips, your sign-in details and your account from the Packr database. If you cannot use the app, ask the person who invited you to Packr to delete your data.

Contact

Packr is invite-only. For questions about this policy, ask the person who invited you to the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
